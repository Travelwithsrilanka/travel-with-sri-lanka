Yala Safari, Sri Lanka

Experience the wild side of Sri Lanka with a Yala National Park safari. Discover elephants, leopards, birds, wildlife and breathtaking natural landscapes.

Plan My Yala Safari

Discover Yala National Park

Yala National Park is one of Sri Lanka's best-known wildlife destinations. Its diverse habitats support elephants, leopards, sloth bears, crocodiles, deer and many species of birds. A safari offers visitors the opportunity to explore these natural landscapes while experiencing Sri Lanka's incredible biodiversity.

🐆 Leopards

Yala is well known for its Sri Lankan leopard population. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, but exploring the park offers exciting opportunities for wildlife enthusiasts.

🐘 Elephants

Look for Sri Lankan elephants moving through grasslands, forests and other habitats within the national park.

🐊 Crocodiles

Lakes and waterways within the park provide habitat for crocodiles and many other wildlife species.

🦚 Birdlife

Yala is home to a wide variety of resident and migratory birds, making it an excellent destination for nature and bird enthusiasts.

What to Expect on a Yala Safari

🚙 Wildlife Drive

Explore the park by safari vehicle and travel through different habitats while looking for wildlife.

🌿 Natural Landscapes

Experience forests, grasslands, lagoons, rocky areas and coastal landscapes.

📸 Wildlife Photography

Bring your camera and capture Sri Lanka's wildlife and spectacular natural scenery.

🌅 Nature Experience

Enjoy an unforgettable experience surrounded by Sri Lanka's wildlife and wilderness.

Responsible Wildlife Tourism

Wildlife should always be observed respectfully. We encourage visitors to follow park regulations, maintain a safe distance from animals, avoid disturbing wildlife and respect Sri Lanka's natural environment.
