Explore Sigiriya

Sigiriya is one of Sri Lanka's most iconic destinations. The ancient rock fortress, surrounded by gardens and dramatic landscapes, offers travelers a unique combination of history, archaeology, nature and culture.

🏛️ Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Explore the ancient rock fortress and discover its remarkable history, gardens, archaeological remains and spectacular views.

⛰️ Pidurangala Rock

Enjoy a memorable hike to Pidurangala Rock and take in panoramic views of the surrounding landscape and Sigiriya Rock.

🌿 Sigiriya Village

Experience rural Sri Lankan life, traditional food, local communities and the peaceful countryside around Sigiriya.

🐘 Wildlife & Nature

Combine your Sigiriya journey with nearby wildlife experiences and discover Sri Lanka's incredible biodiversity.
