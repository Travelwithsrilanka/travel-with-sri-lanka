10 Famous Places in Sigiriya

Sigiriya Rock Fortress

One of Sri Lanka's most famous ancient landmarks and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Sigiriya Museum

Learn about the history, archaeology and ancient civilization of Sigiriya.

Sigiriya Frescoes

Famous ancient paintings preserved on the rock fortress.

Mirror Wall

A historic wall containing ancient visitor inscriptions.

Water Gardens

Beautiful ancient gardens featuring ponds, fountains and sophisticated hydraulic systems.

Boulder Gardens

A fascinating area of massive rocks and ancient pathways around Sigiriya.

Pidurangala Rock

A famous viewpoint offering spectacular views towards Sigiriya Rock.

Dambulla Cave Temple

An ancient cave temple complex filled with Buddhist paintings and statues.

Minneriya National Park

A famous wildlife destination known for large gatherings of elephants.

An atmospheric ancient monastery hidden within a beautiful forest landscape.
