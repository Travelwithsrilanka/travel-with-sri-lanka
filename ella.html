Explore Ella, Sri Lanka

Ella is one of Sri Lanka's most scenic mountain destinations. Surrounded by lush green hills, tea plantations and dramatic valleys, Ella is perfect for travellers looking for nature, adventure and unforgettable views.

With Travel With Sri Lanka, you can explore Ella at your own pace, visit its famous landmarks, experience local culture and discover some of the most beautiful scenery in Sri Lanka.

Ella Rock

A spectacular hiking destination offering panoramic views across Ella's mountain landscape.

Nine Arch Bridge

One of Ella's most iconic landmarks, surrounded by lush tropical forest and tea-covered hills.

Little Adam's Peak

A relatively accessible hike with beautiful views over Ella's surrounding valleys and mountains.

🌿 Nature Mountains, forests, waterfalls and tea plantations.
🥾 Adventure Hiking, sightseeing and scenic outdoor experiences.
🚆 Scenic Travel Experience some of Sri Lanka's most beautiful railway scenery.

Why Visit Ella?

Ella combines adventure and relaxation in one destination. Visitors can spend the morning hiking through the hills, explore tea plantations during the day and enjoy spectacular sunset views in the evening.

Ella is also an excellent destination for couples, families, solo travellers and photographers looking to experience the natural beauty of Sri Lanka.
