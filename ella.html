Ella, Sri Lanka

Discover breathtaking mountains, waterfalls, tea plantations, scenic railway journeys and unforgettable experiences in beautiful Ella.

Discover the Beauty of Ella

Ella is one of Sri Lanka's most beautiful mountain destinations. From dramatic viewpoints and waterfalls to historic railway landmarks and tea plantations, Ella offers unforgettable experiences for every traveler.

10 Famous Places in Ella

Explore some of the most famous attractions and experiences around Ella, Sri Lanka.

01 • ELLA

Ella Rock

A spectacular hiking destination offering breathtaking views across Ella's mountains, valleys and surrounding countryside.

02 • ELLA

Nine Arch Bridge

One of Sri Lanka's most photographed railway bridges, surrounded by lush green jungle and beautiful mountain scenery.

03 • ELLA

Little Adam's Peak

A popular and relatively easy hike offering panoramic views over Ella's dramatic mountain landscape.

04 • ELLA

Ravana Falls

A spectacular waterfall near Ella surrounded by dramatic rock formations and tropical mountain scenery.

05 • ELLA

Ravana Cave

An atmospheric cave associated with the legendary stories surrounding King Ravana.

06 • ELLA

Lipton's Seat

A famous viewpoint surrounded by spectacular tea estates and panoramic mountain scenery.

07 • ELLA

Diyaluma Falls

One of Sri Lanka's spectacular waterfalls, famous for its natural pools and impressive mountain scenery.

08 • ELLA

Demodara Railway Loop

A unique railway engineering landmark where the railway line makes a remarkable loop through the mountain landscape.

09 • ELLA

Ella Spice Garden

Discover Sri Lankan spices, herbs and local culinary traditions in a relaxing garden setting.

10 • ELLA

Uva Halpewatte Tea Factory

Experience Sri Lanka's famous tea culture and learn about the production of Ceylon Tea.
