Ella, Sri Lanka

Discover breathtaking mountains, waterfalls, tea plantations and unforgettable experiences in beautiful Ella.

10 Famous Places in Ella

Ella Rock

A spectacular hiking destination offering beautiful mountain and valley views.

Nine Arch Bridge

One of Sri Lanka's most photographed railway bridges surrounded by lush greenery.

Little Adam's Peak

A relatively easy hike with panoramic views over Ella's mountains.

Ravana Falls

A dramatic waterfall located close to Ella and associated with the famous Ravana legend.

Ravana Cave

An atmospheric cave associated with the legendary stories of King Ravana.

Lipton's Seat

A famous viewpoint surrounded by spectacular tea estates and mountain scenery.

Diyaluma Falls

One of Sri Lanka's spectacular waterfalls, famous for its natural pools and views.

Demodara Railway Loop

A unique railway engineering attraction near Ella.

Ella Spice Garden

Learn about Sri Lankan spices, herbs and local culinary traditions.

Uva Halpewatte Tea Factory

Experience Sri Lanka's tea culture and learn how famous Ceylon Tea is produced.
