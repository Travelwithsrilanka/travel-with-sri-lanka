Explore Ella

Ella is one of Sri Lanka's most spectacular mountain destinations. Surrounded by green hills, tea plantations and dramatic valleys, it is an ideal destination for travelers looking for nature, adventure, culture and relaxation.

Ella Rock

Enjoy a memorable hike to Ella Rock and experience panoramic views across Sri Lanka's beautiful hill country.

Nine Arch Bridge

Visit the famous Nine Arch Bridge and watch Sri Lanka's iconic blue trains travel through the surrounding jungle.

Little Adam's Peak

Take an easy scenic hike to Little Adam's Peak for beautiful views of Ella's surrounding mountains.

Tea Plantations

Explore Sri Lanka's tea country, discover local tea production and enjoy the peaceful scenery of the highlands.

Things to Do in Ella

🌿 Nature & Hiking

Explore mountain trails, waterfalls, forests and breathtaking viewpoints.

🚂 Scenic Train Journey

Experience one of Sri Lanka's most beautiful train journeys through the hill country.

☕ Tea Experience

Discover the history and culture of Sri Lanka's famous Ceylon tea.

Capture stunning mountain landscapes, railway scenery, waterfalls and local life.
