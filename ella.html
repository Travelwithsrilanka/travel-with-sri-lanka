Explore Ella, Sri Lanka

Ella is one of Sri Lanka's most beautiful hill-country destinations. Surrounded by green mountains, tea plantations and spectacular viewpoints, Ella is perfect for travelers looking for nature, adventure and scenic experiences.

Popular experiences include visiting Ella Rock, the Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak, tea plantations and the surrounding waterfalls and mountain landscapes.

Why Visit Ella?

Nature • Adventure • Mountain Views • Tea Plantations • Scenic Travel
