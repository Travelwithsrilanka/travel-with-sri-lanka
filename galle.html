Galle, Sri Lanka

Discover historic Galle Fort, beautiful beaches, colonial architecture and the unique culture of Sri Lanka's southern coast.

Explore Galle

Galle is one of Sri Lanka's most fascinating coastal destinations. Its historic fort, beautiful ocean views, charming streets and nearby beaches make it an excellent destination for travelers interested in history, culture, photography and relaxation.

🏰 Galle Fort

Walk through the historic Galle Fort and discover its old walls, architecture, streets, shops and ocean views.

🌊 Southern Beaches

Relax on the beautiful beaches along Sri Lanka's southern coastline and enjoy the tropical atmosphere.

🏛️ Colonial Architecture

Discover the unique blend of Sri Lankan, Dutch and Portuguese influences visible throughout the historic area.

🌴 Coastal Villages

Experience local life, traditional fishing, food and the peaceful atmosphere of Sri Lanka's southern coastal communities.

Things to Do in Galle

🏰 Explore Galle Fort

Walk around the historic fort, discover landmarks and enjoy spectacular views over the Indian Ocean.

📸 Photography

Capture historic buildings, narrow streets, ocean views, sunsets and colorful local life.

🏖️ Beach Experience

Combine your Galle visit with a relaxing day along the beautiful southern coastline.

🍛 Local Food

Taste authentic Sri Lankan cuisine and discover local flavors from the southern region.

Galle and the South Coast
