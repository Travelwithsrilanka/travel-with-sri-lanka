Galle, Sri Lanka

Explore colonial history, beautiful beaches, coastal landscapes and the iconic Galle Fort.

10 Famous Places in Galle

Galle Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage historic fortress overlooking the Indian Ocean.

Galle Lighthouse

An iconic landmark standing beside the historic fort walls.

Dutch Reformed Church

A historic church located inside Galle Fort.

Maritime Museum

Discover the maritime history of Sri Lanka's southern coast.

Unawatuna Beach

A popular tropical beach famous for swimming, sunsets and coastal scenery.

Jungle Beach

A secluded beach surrounded by tropical vegetation near Unawatuna.

Japanese Peace Pagoda

A peaceful hilltop landmark offering beautiful coastal views.

Dalawella Beach

A beautiful coastal location known for its relaxed tropical atmosphere.

Koggala Lake

A scenic lagoon surrounded by lush greenery and small islands.

Martin Wickramasinghe Museum

A cultural museum celebrating the life and work of a famous Sri Lankan writer.
