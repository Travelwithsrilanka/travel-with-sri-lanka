Explore Kandy

Kandy is a beautiful hill-country city famous for its cultural heritage, sacred sites, Kandy Lake and surrounding natural scenery.

Visitors can explore the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, enjoy the peaceful atmosphere around Kandy Lake and discover the beautiful Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya.
