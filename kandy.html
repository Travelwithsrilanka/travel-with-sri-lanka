Kandy, Sri Lanka

Discover Sri Lanka's cultural heart, historic temples, beautiful lake, traditional experiences and spectacular hill-country scenery.

Plan My Kandy Trip

Explore Kandy

Kandy is one of Sri Lanka's most important cultural destinations. Surrounded by green hills and centered around the beautiful Kandy Lake, the city offers travelers history, spirituality, culture, architecture and memorable local experiences.

🛕 Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

Visit one of Sri Lanka's most significant Buddhist religious sites and discover its rich cultural heritage.

🌊 Kandy Lake

Enjoy the peaceful scenery around Kandy Lake and experience the atmosphere of the historic city center.

🌿 Royal Botanic Gardens

Explore beautiful tropical gardens, exotic plants, trees and peaceful walking paths near Kandy.

🥁 Cultural Experiences

Experience traditional Sri Lankan dance, music, crafts and local culture during your Kandy journey.

Things to Do in Kandy

🛕 Discover Sri Lankan Culture

Explore historic temples, traditional customs and important cultural landmarks.

🌳 Explore Nature

Enjoy the green landscapes, botanical gardens and surrounding mountains of Sri Lanka's hill country.

🎭 Watch Cultural Performances

Experience traditional Sri Lankan dance and cultural performances.

📸 Photography

Capture temples, lake views, mountain landscapes, gardens and everyday local life.

Kandy to Other Sri Lanka Destinations

Kandy is also an excellent stop when traveling through Sri Lanka's central highlands. Your journey can be combined with destinations such as Sigiriya, Nuwara Eliya and Ella depending on your itinerary.
