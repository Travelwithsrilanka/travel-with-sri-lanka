Udawalawe National Park

One of Sri Lanka's most rewarding wildlife destinations

Udawalawe is famous for its open landscapes, abundant wildlife and large herds of Asian elephants. It is an excellent destination for travelers who want to combine nature, photography and an exciting Sri Lankan safari experience.

Travel With Sri Lanka can help you plan a personalized Udawalawe experience as part of a wider Sri Lanka journey.

Why Visit Udawalawe?

Wildlife encounters and unforgettable natural experiences

🐘 Asian Elephants

Udawalawe is particularly well known for its elephant population, making it one of the best places in Sri Lanka to observe these magnificent animals in their natural environment.

🦅 Bird Watching

The park and surrounding wetlands provide opportunities to see many species of resident and migratory birds.

🐊 Wildlife

Depending on the season and conditions, visitors may encounter crocodiles, water buffalo, deer, monkeys and many other species.

📸 Photography

Open landscapes and abundant wildlife make Udawalawe an excellent destination for wildlife photography and nature enthusiasts.
